To create a vibrant literary bridge that connects global masterpieces with Sri Lankan readers while elevating local voices to international standards, enriching culture and imagination through the power of storytelling.

To discover, develop, and promote authentic Sri Lankan authors while collaborating with international publishers to deliver high-quality translated works, producing books that combine editorial excellence, cultural relevance, and premium craftsmanship for readers.
